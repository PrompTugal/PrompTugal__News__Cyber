Explore the data

Everything on this page runs in your browser. It reads the published JSON from data.promptugal.news (CISA's known-exploited list and the latest NVD window) and from this site, then draws it, lets you browse every record and query it with SQL (SQLite, running locally). Nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

Worldwide data. Counts reflect the activity of public catalogues, not how secure anyone is. Weak signals, not an audit. Product and vendor names only: no hosts, IPs or victims.

Starting…

The vendors with most entries in CISA's known-exploited list, linked to the weakness types (CWE) of their CVEs; a thicker line means more CVEs. Click a vendor, a weakness or a line between them to see those CVEs.

Tables: kev(cve, vendor, product, name, date_added, due_date, ransomware, cwes), kev_cwe(cve, cwe), nvd(cve, published, last_modified, status, cna, cvss_version, score, severity, products_from), nvd_product(cve, vendor, product), kev_weekly(week, total, partial). SQLite runs in your browser; Ctrl+Enter runs.
