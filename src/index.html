The pulse of Portugal's cyber posture

How exposed are the products and vendors that Portugal relies on, and how does that change week by week? This site will answer with totals from public vulnerability sources, kept as a history, so that the trend shows over time.

The first figures, from 18 November 2026: worldwide totals from CISA's known-exploited list and NVD, read from data.promptugal.news. They become a pulse for Portugal once the list of vendors relevant to Portugal is published. Every figure here is also JSON: /api/v1/pulse/index.json.

What comes next

  • The Portugal lens: the same counts for vendors and products relevant to Portugal, from a published list with its reasons (CISA's known-exploited list, NVD).
  • New CVEs, week by week: whole weeks need whole-day counts from NVD, asked of data.b@; until then, each 24-hour read is kept as it is.
  • The European picture: ENISA's published reports, on hold until Dinis decides.
  • Proposed, not yet built: totals by sector (energy, health, finance, public administration, telecoms). No source gives sector directly, so this needs our own published mapping of products to sectors first.

The list of vendors relevant to Portugal will be published with its reasons, and will hold vendor and product names only, never their customers.

What it is not

These are weak signals from public sources, not an audit. A count of vulnerabilities is not a measure of how well any organisation is protected. We show totals only: never a list of IPs, hosts, devices or exposed services, never a victim, never a link to a leak site. We scan and probe nothing ourselves. The full limits are on How it works.
